# DeskPi RackMate T2

## Overview

The DeskPi RackMate T2 serves as the primary rack for Project Atlas.

Its compact footprint provides sufficient room for enterprise-style hardware while fitting into a small office environment.

---

# Installed Equipment

- Datto Proxmox Host
- Mediasonic ProRAID
- TP-Link Managed Switch

---

# Build Process

## Assembly

- Rack assembled
- Shelves installed
- Hardware mounted
- Initial cable routing completed

---

# Cable Management

Power and network cables were separated where practical.

Future improvements include:

- Cable labels
- Velcro straps
- UPS integration

---

# Lessons Learned

- Community 3D printed rack ears required modification.
- Always verify dimensions before printing.
- Assemble the rack before routing cables.
